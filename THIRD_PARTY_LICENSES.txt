SLOPOS-I third-party notices
============================

SLOPOS-I first-party code and original assets are MIT-licensed. Third-party
Rust crates, fonts, Linux components, drivers, firmware, codecs and services
remain owned by their respective authors and retain their own licenses. The
repository-level LICENSE does not relicense them.

Release procedure
-----------------

Before publishing binaries, run the locked dependency audit and generate a
machine-derived attribution bundle from the exact dependency graph:

    cargo deny check licenses advisories bans sources

Use a tool such as cargo-about or cargo-license for the release bundle. Preserve
all notices required by direct and transitive dependencies. Do not maintain a
guessed crate inventory in project documentation.

System substrate
----------------

Externally supplied components may include the Linux kernel, X.Org-compatible
X11 server, Openbox, Mesa, PipeWire, NetworkManager, BlueZ, UDisks2,
systemd/logind and installed fonts. They are not covered by the SLOPOS-I MIT
grant. SLOPOS-I does not ship model weights or require a Vision/AI runtime.
